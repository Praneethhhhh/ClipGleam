# Why We Get Seasons

Status: Proposed original reference-quality video. Review before generation.

## Creative direction

A curious kid packs for July, then learns why a friend in the other hemisphere needs different clothes. The contradiction makes the distance explanation fail in one picture. A flashlight experiment makes the actual explanation visible. One Earth globe and its axis carry through the animation. The camera changes scale from split-screen live-action-style illustrations to an overhead orbit, a tabletop experiment, and a rotating globe.

Vertical 9:16. Target 90 seconds, delivered at a natural, energetic pace. Show the first visual gag by second two. The following times are editorial allocations. Final cuts and captions follow actual speech timing. Change the shot or the active visual inside the shot when the narration asks the viewer to notice something new.
